John McLevey

Professor & Head, Sociology & Criminology
Memorial University · St. John's NL

Hi! I'm John (he/him). I'm Professor and Head of the Department of Sociology & Criminology at Memorial University. Before moving to Memorial, I completed a PhD in Sociology at McMaster University (2013) and was a faculty member at the University of Waterloo for 11 years (2013–2024).

I work in computational social science, network science, cultural cognition and affect, and quantitative methodology — with substantive interests in political sociology, sociology of science, and environmental social science. My research has been funded by SSHRC and an Early Researcher Award from the Ontario Ministry of Research and Innovation.
